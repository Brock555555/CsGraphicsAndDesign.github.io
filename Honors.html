FFA State Degree - 2022
The FFA State Degree is the highest honor a state FFA association can award, requiring at least 2 years of membership, the Chapter FFA Degree, 360 hours of agricultural education, a strong SAE project with at least $2,000 earned or 600 hours worked, leadership activities, parliamentary procedure skills, a 6‑minute speech, and at least 25 hours of community service.

FFA Chapter Degree - 2019
The Chapter FFA Degree is awarded to FFA members who have demonstrated active participation, leadership, and a supervised agricultural experience at the chapter level.

FFA Greenhand Degree - 2018
The Greenhand FFA Degree is the first official degree awarded to new FFA members, recognizing their knowledge of FFA history, mission, and basic agricultural skills.
